# Events & meetups

AP77 hosts **social events and lifestyle meetups** in Australia’s major cities. Formats include mixers, community nights, and curated local experiences.

## Cities

### Sydney

Harbour-city socials and lifestyle meetups — ideal for networking casually, meeting new people, or enjoying a planned night out with a welcoming group.

### Melbourne

Culture-forward gatherings and community evenings that suit Melbourne’s social calendar — from easygoing mixers to themed lifestyle nights.

### Brisbane

Warm, approachable city socials and informal meetups designed for connection without the pressure of a formal scene.

## How it works

1. Check the latest listings on [ap77.space](https://ap77.space/) or [ap77club.com](https://ap77club.com/).
2. Choose your city and event.
3. Complete RSVP / registration via the official links on those sites.
4. Arrive on time, say hello, and enjoy the night.

Always use official AP77 channels for dates, venues, dress codes (if any), and capacity limits.

## What to expect

- Friendly, community-first atmosphere
- Clear event details published in advance
- Opportunities to meet locals and newcomers
- Lifestyle-oriented social formats (not gambling or casino entertainment)

## Stay updated

For new dates and member-priority updates, see [Membership](membership.md) or join via [ap77club.com](https://ap77club.com/).
